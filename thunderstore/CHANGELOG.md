# Changelog

## 0.6.6 (beta)
- First release on Thunderstore, as Hades 2 Competitive Speedrunning (H2CS), a beta.
- Installed with the script before? It moves your bests, loadout and name to the new
  mod folder (schmidtwmark-Hades_2_Competitive_Speedrunning) and removes the old one.

## 0.6.5
- How to Update points mod-manager installs at their mod manager.

## 0.6.4
- Keepsakes that grant something random at the start (Jeweled Pom's Hades boon, the
  temporary Daedalus Hammer, the Chaos blessing) grant the same thing to every racer.
- Starting a race with the board's chat page open no longer leaves the pause menu's
  Forfeit and Race Menu unresponsive.
- Under the race clock: your best split for the region you're in.

## 0.6.3
- Your name in races is your Steam (or Epic) name.
- Personal bests per route, kept by the race server with your account and shown on the
  route vote for everyone in the race.

## 0.6.2
- No log window at startup.
- Competitive mode stays in the training grounds.
- Olympus's eagle no longer plays its first-sighting slow motion.
- The race server keeps every race's record for fairness checks.

## 0.6.1
- All three Erebus minibosses can come up, and bosses use their full movesets.
- Button labels fit their buttons.

## 0.6.0
- Find Match pairs two racers; everyone readies to vote, no countdown.
- Add Bot in a private race.
- Each vote shows what the earlier ones picked.
- The Race Board says when a newer version is out.

## 0.5.x
- Races of two to five, the same offers for every racer, routes, prep time, quick chat,
  region splits and personal bests, and many fixes from real races.
